# Legal Review Notes

Status: Draft for legal review before production.

These documents are engineering and product-risk drafts, not legal advice. Before production, qualified counsel should review:

- Business legal identity and registration details.
- Governing law and consumer-protection obligations.
- No-refund wording and legally required carveouts.
- Privacy-law obligations for Kenya, EU/UK, and international customers.
- Data retention periods for bookings, ledgers, receipts, logs, and support.
- Payment-provider terms and M-Pesa/Daraja disclosure requirements.
- Breach notification and customer rights request process.
- Cookie/analytics consent if analytics are added later.

Do not add fake registration numbers, fake addresses, or claims of lawyer approval.
