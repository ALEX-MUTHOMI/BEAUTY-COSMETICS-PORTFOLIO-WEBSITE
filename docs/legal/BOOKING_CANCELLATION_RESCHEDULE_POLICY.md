## Confirmation

Payment confirms a booking only after checkout provider evidence is validated and the internal billing ledger records success. Pending, failed, expired, cancelled, or manual-review payments do not automatically confirm a booking.

## No Refund

Paid bookings are non-refundable. Rescheduling may be available according to the Booking Policy. Nothing in this policy limits rights that cannot legally be excluded under applicable law.

## Rescheduling

Reschedules are subject to the configured cutoff period and maximum reschedule count. Normal booking and reschedule times are Monday through Saturday, 07:00 <= start < 19:00, Africa/Nairobi. Sunday normal bookings and reschedules are rejected unless a future urgent-booking policy is explicitly implemented.

## Late Arrival And No-Show

Late arrival grace periods and no-show handling are business policy controls and must be displayed before production. No-show handling does not create an automatic refund path.

## Manual Review

Late, expired, mismatched, duplicate, or provider-delayed payment events may be routed to manual review. Manual review must not double-credit the billing ledger.
